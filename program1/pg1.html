About Us

The Kerala Muslim Educational Association (KMEA) was established way back in the year 1957, with the noble aim of setting up Educational and Social Institutions, promoting Education and Training among all sections of the Community, by providing financial assistance to deserving students. The late Shri. Poker Sahib, an eminent Parliamentarian and Philanthropist, and late Shri. K. M. Seethi Sahib, the former speaker of the Kerala Legislative Assembly, was the key force behind the KMEA movement. The former Chief Minister of Kerala, the late Shri C. H. Mohammed Koya, had also actively participated in the formation and promotion of this Association. The Association aims to promote Quality Education and Philanthropy, with a special emphasis on uplifting the downtrodden classes and empowering the weaker sections of society. At present KMEA runs the KMEA Engineering College, College of Arts and Science, and KMEA College of Architecture, all in the Kuzhivelipady Campus, Seethi Sahib Memorial Polytechnic College at Tirur, Malappuram and Abdulla Haji Ahmed Sait Memorial KMEA Al Manar Higher Secondary School, Kuzhivelippady, Edathala, Aluva. Ever since of establishment, KMEA has been extending Financial Assistance and Scholarships to the needy, meritorious and talented students, for their Higher Education, especially in the Professional and Technical Fields. KMEA has been providing Scholarships and Stipends, amounting to more than one crore of rupees, during the last five years. In addition to Scholarships and Stipends, the Association implements a “FEE WAIVER SCHEME”, each year, in all Educational institutions, among all classes of students, irrespective of Caste, Creed, Religion and Gender.
